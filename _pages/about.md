I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the **HKUST NLP Group** at the **Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)**, where I am supervised by Professor Junxian He. My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**. I received my **B.Eng.** degree from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024, where I was also advised by Professor Junxian He.
